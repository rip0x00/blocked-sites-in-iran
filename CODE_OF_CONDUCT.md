# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant. In short:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique data, methods and code — not
  people.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind, in issues, pull
  requests, or any project space.
- Assume good faith: reports come from many networks and countries, and
  honest measurements can disagree.
- Keep discussions on-topic and evidence-based.

The repository owner moderates discussions and may remove comments, close
issues, or block participants who violate these rules. Decisions can be
appealed by messaging the owner on Telegram: https://t.me/rip00
